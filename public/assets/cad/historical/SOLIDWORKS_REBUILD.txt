HOW TO BUILD IT IN SOLIDWORKS  (about 20 minutes)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Create a PART named  01_Outer_Shell

1. Front Plane > Sketch
   - Centreline on the vertical axis.
   - Rectangle from Y=80 to Y=580, X=147 to X=150
     (this is the 3 mm wall of the 300 mm OD cylinder).
2. Revolve Boss/Base 360° about the centreline.
   You now have the outer absorber shell.

Create a PART named  02_Inner_Shell

3. Front Plane > Sketch
   - Rectangle from Y=95 to Y=565, X=68 to X=70.
4. Revolve 360°.
5. Hole Wizard or Cut-Extrude:
   - On the outer face of the inner tube, sketch a 4 mm circle
     at Y=120, sitting on the surface.
   - Cut-Extrude Through All (or Up To Next into the bore).
6. Circular Pattern: 18 instances about the tube axis.
7. Linear Pattern the circular set up the axis: 10 instances, 40 mm spacing.
   This is the perforated inner shell.

Create a PART named  03_Hopper

8. Front Plane > Sketch a trapezoid:
   - Outer profile from (150,580) to (90,700) and inner from (147,580) to (40,700).
9. Revolve 360°.

Create a PART named  04_Discharge

10. Front Plane > Sketch a trapezoid:
    - Outer from (35,30) to (150,80) and inner from (33,30) to (147,80).
11. Revolve 360°.

Create a PART named  05_Stand

12. Sketch a 280×280 square on Top Plane.
13. Offset inward 25 mm. Extrude the frame 25 mm (bottom rail).
14. Extrude four 25×25 legs 80 mm high at the corners.
15. Copy the 280×280 frame at Z=55 (top rail).

Create an ASSEMBLY named  Hollow_Cylinder_Paddy_Dryer

16. Insert 05_Stand on the origin.
17. Mate 04_Discharge concentric + coincident on the stand top.
18. Mate 01_Outer_Shell concentric to Discharge, coincident on the
    discharge upper flange.
19. Mate 02_Inner_Shell concentric to Outer_Shell, 15 mm up from
    the shell floor so grain cannot fall into a closed bottom.
20. Mate 03_Hopper concentric + coincident on the top of Outer_Shell.

Optional appearance
- Outer shell: dark grey / black (absorber coating).
- Inner shell: light steel.
- Stand: dark steel.

----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS MODEL IS / IS NOT
----------------------------------------------------------------
IS
- A rebuildable feature tree you can take to your supervisor.
- A geometry to measure surface area for a first energy-balance.
- A cutting-list source (unroll the cylinders in SolidWorks Sheet Metal
  later if you switch the outer shell to a rolled sheet).

IS NOT
- A proven dryer.
- A final hole pattern (4 mm / 18 / 10 is only so the model looks right).
- A mill. Keep the dehusker as a separate part later.
